Proposal Document Format

Your proposal must be a concise 1–1.5 page PDF structured into the following five required sections:

1. Introduction

  • Problem Statement: Clearly describe the core technical or scientific problem you intend to solve.
  • Goal: What specific capability, system behavior, or performance milestone will your artifact accomplish?
  • Context & Datasets/Assets: Define your data source, media corpus, or execution pipeline. Clarify whether this is an investigation of novelty (Research) or utility (Application).

2. Background & Related Work

  • Summarize relevant prior work, foundational literature, or existing reference implementations.
  • Requirement: At least 3 citations/references.
  • Requirement: At least one archival/peer-reviewed source (ACM, IEEE, NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, SIGGRAPH, etc.).

3. Methodology

  • Baselines: What existing method, model architecture, or reference system will serve as your comparative baseline?
  • Proposed Approach: What novel algorithm, pipeline modification, or system integration will you implement?
  • Technical Specifics: Detail your software stack, libraries, engines, training objectives, or algorithms. Avoid vague assertions.

4. Evaluation Plan

  • Quantitative Metrics: Accuracy, F1-score, throughput, latency benchmarks, render times, or statistical convergence rates.
  • Qualitative Assessment: Visual fidelity audits, error analysis, or comparative side-by-side walkthroughs.
  • Validation Rigor: Train/test splits, cross-validation protocols, or controlled baseline comparison runs.

5. Milestones & Timeline

  • List 3–6 dated objectives (e.g., {{DATE_MILESTONE_EX}}: Baseline Pipeline & Ingestion Framework Established).
